## Business Problem

Sales teams had thousands of CRM contacts but limited visibility into which contacts were actually usable for prospecting.

The objective was to identify:

- Which contacts are reachable
- Which accounts lack usable decision makers
- Which job-title profiles are associated with opportunities
- Where CRM remediation should be prioritized

## Dataset

The model combines:

- Accounts
- Contacts
- Sales activities
- Opportunities
- Contracts

All portfolio data has been anonymised or synthetically generated.

## Key KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Contact Completeness | Measures whether required CRM attributes exist |
| Confirmed Reachability | Contacts with validated communication channels |
| Campaign Ready Contacts | Contacts suitable for activation |
| Decision Maker Coverage | Accounts with target personas |
| Activity Recency | Measures recent sales engagement |

## Analysis

### 1. Contact Health

Identified contacts that were complete but not actually reachable.

### 2. Decision Maker Coverage

Segmented contacts into CFO, Finance Director, CEO and other
decision-maker profiles.

### 3. Sales Activation

Connected CRM quality with sales opportunities to understand which
contact profiles were most associated with successful outcomes.
